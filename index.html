The Verdict
Five headline findings, each with a number.
1
CPL improved 31% in one month, from $364.22 to $251.15, with form completion near 20% against a 12 to 15% benchmark.
2
The named ICP accounts we most want, payers and brokers like Optum, UnitedHealth, Cigna and Gallagher, engage at 3 to 5% CTR against a 1.06% account average.
3
The two highest-CTR creatives in the account are durability document ads at 7.6 and 8.5% CTR, proving the positioning thesis in-market.
4
The personas run blended across every company size in broad campaigns. The data shows distinct hero segments (enterprise VP and CXO clinical buyers versus mid-market benefits leaders) worth isolating with dedicated creative and budget.
5
The warm pools are saturating (Follower Ads at frequency 30, 49 to 71% penetration) while cold ICP is 85 to 92% unreached. That gap is the bridge to the next channel.
