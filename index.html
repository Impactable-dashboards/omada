Creative Direction
A standing, explicit ask from the client. Guardrails from the rolling creative review.

Imagery

ELT executive headshots on minimalist backgrounds outperform abstract imagery, the clearest repeatable finding. Lifestyle imagery earns its place only in BOF member stories.

Color

Omada orange works as a typography and headline accent, not a background fill. The Proof Points style (data point plus orange accent) is a consistent top performer.

Copy

Lead with a specific metric in the first 100 words. 80 to 120 word intros with line breaks beat long form. Clinical and persistence framing wins; retire Flex Care and abstract launch messaging.

Company and Strategy

Omada Health is a virtual-first provider of between-visit cardiometabolic care, pairing human care teams with AI. Strictly a B2B buyer, never member-facing, positioned to complement primary care, not replace it. The engagement has built an awareness foundation and executive thought-leadership engine; the strategy this quarter is to convert that foundation into measurable demand.
